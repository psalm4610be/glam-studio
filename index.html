Privacy Policy

Glam Studio is designed to be a privacy-focused business app. We do not collect, sell, or share your personal data with third parties.

Data storage Stored locally on your device
Server sync No server sync used
Tracking No analytics or ad tracking
Backups Saved only to the location you choose

What information the app stores

Glam Studio may store information you manually add into the app, including client records, appointments, project details, message templates, service packages, expenses, receipts, notes, and reminder settings.

Where your data lives

All app data is stored locally on your device. If you create a backup, that backup is saved only in the place you explicitly choose, such as iCloud Drive or On My iPhone.

What the app does not do

  • It does not upload your business data to our servers.
  • It does not sell or share your personal information.
  • It does not use third-party advertising or hidden analytics tools.

Permissions

Some features may request permission only when needed:

  • Contacts to import clients from your address book.
  • Notifications to send reminders for appointments and backups.
  • Photos to attach receipts or images to your records.
  • Files to import or export backups and documents you choose.
In short: Glam Studio is built to help manage your business while keeping your data under your control.

App usage

  • You may use Glam Studio to manage clients, appointments, projects, services, reminders, and business records.
  • You are responsible for reviewing, editing, and maintaining the accuracy of the information you enter.
  • You are responsible for creating backups if you want to protect your data from accidental loss.

No warranty

Backups and responsibility

The app includes local backup options, but you remain responsible for saving and keeping copies of your backup files in a location you trust.

Subscriptions

If Glam Studio offers a subscription, it will automatically renew unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current billing period. Subscriptions can be managed in your Apple ID account settings.
